# Ideador Viral Copy DNA Pattern

## 1. Purpose
The **Ideador Viral** module produces high-retention e-commerce and UGC video ideas with concise, highly persuasive, short-form copy tailored for platforms like TikTok, TikTok Shop, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

---

## 2. Preserved DNA & Schema Contract

Every idea generated by this pattern strictly adheres to a flat JSON structure containing exactly **5 concepts** per request:

```json
[
  {
    "title": "Explanatory, direct concept title",
    "angle": "Psychological sales angle (e.g., Prova Social, Unboxing Bizarro, Curiosidade Oculta, Inveja Saudável)",
    "hook": "Spoken hook with at most 15 words in natural spoken language",
    "sceneConcept": "Visual detail of what occurs in the first 3 seconds on screen",
    "productUse": "How the featured product is organically and natively introduced",
    "cta": "Direct closing call-to-action focused on fast action (link, cart, bio)",
    "suggestedVisualPromptEn": "Descriptive, cinematic visual prompt in technical English for Veo, Sora, Runway, or Kling",
    "dialoguePtBr": "Natural creator-style spoken script in PT-BR (maximum 2-3 sentences)",
    "why": "Logical explanation of why this script captures attention and converts"
  }
]
```

---

## 3. Core Performance Rules

1. **Word Budget on Hook**: The spoken hook **must have at most 15 words**.
2. **Dialogue Budget**: The spoken dialogue (`dialoguePtBr`) **must have at most 2–3 short sentences**.
3. **Anti-Cliché Directive**: Explicit ban on marketing clichés and artificial infomercial phrases (e.g., *"preço de banana"*, *"revolucionário"*, *"não perca essa oportunidade"*).
4. **First 3 Seconds Synchronization (`sceneConcept`)**: Must clearly describe the visual scene taking place in the first 3 seconds, matching the spoken hook.
5. **Organic Product Introduction (`productUse`)**: The product must be woven naturally into the scene rather than presented as a traditional television commercial.
6. **English Visual Prompt (`suggestedVisualPromptEn`)**: Written in technical English, detailed and cinematic, ready for video diffusion models.
7. **Strategic Coherence Anchor (`why`)**: Explains the psychological mechanism driving retention and sales.

---

## 4. Why It Works

* **Short Constraints Prevent Hallucinatory Rambling**: Hard length limits prevent LLMs from writing verbose monologues that destroy short-form retention.
* **Audio-Visual Synchronization**: Pairing `hook` (spoken audio) with `sceneConcept` (first 3 seconds visual action) ensures immediate viewer hook rate.
* **Native UGC Delivery**: Enforcing `productUse` and `dialoguePtBr` maintains a creator-style delivery that blends with native feed content.
* **Psychological Justification**: Requiring the `why` field forces the model to maintain strategic reasoning throughout generation.

---

## 5. Identified Risks & Boundary Warnings

* **Product Fact Hallucination**: If the user inputs a generic niche without specific details, the AI may invent colors, ingredients, technical specifications, or claims.
* **Invented Offers**: Without strict parameters, the AI may invent fake discounts, artificial scarcity (*"restam apenas 3 unidades"*), or fabricated guarantees.
* **Absence of Fact Lock**: The Ideador Viral currently does not have a separate fact-checking filter; outputs should be reviewed by users for regulatory compliance before publishing.
* **Prompt Dilution Risk**: Adding lengthy system prompts or conflicting instructions will degrade the punchiness and brevity of the generated copy.

---

## 6. Reuse Guidance

This Copy DNA pattern can be referenced and adapted safely in:
* **Storyboard UGC Agent**: For generating frame-by-frame takes with 3-second visual cues and spoken creator dialogue.
* **Creative Director**: For establishing the overarching psychological angle and hook hooks.
* **Script Refiner**: For enforcing word caps (max 15 words on hook, max 2-3 sentences per scene) and stripping corporate clichés.
* **CTA Engine**: For high-conversion, organic e-commerce call-to-actions.
* **Product-to-Video Workflows**: For translating static product attributes into native scene actions.

---

## 7. Protection Rules

* **DO NOT** modify `src/components/IdeationView.tsx` simply to "enrich" or "modernize" prompts.
* **DO NOT** expand the original Ideador prompt with bloated instruction blocks or multi-step chains.
* **DO NOT** alter the JSON schema keys (`title`, `angle`, `hook`, `sceneConcept`, `productUse`, `cta`, `suggestedVisualPromptEn`, `dialoguePtBr`, `why`) without formal migrations.
* **DO NOT** refactor the module unless addressing a confirmed, reproducible bug.
